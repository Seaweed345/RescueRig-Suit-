This website is for information about the RescueRig Suit™.

The RescueRig Suit™ is a modification of the PartRig Suit™. This special modification is designed for rescue workers.

The RescueRig Suit™ adds built-in flashlights, radios, and an energy system. There is a tank of water attached to the exoskeleton; there is a generator attached to the tank. The RescueRig Suit™ can also protect workers from potential injury.

The price is 130$. This price subject to tax. Tax may vary depending on region.
